### Hallucination Cascade: Analyzing Error Propagation in Multi-Agent LLM Systems

- **arXiv:** `2606.07937` — https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07937
- **Authors:** Saeid Jamshidi, Arghavan Moradi Dakhel, Kawser Wazed Nafi, Foutse Khomh
- **Year:** 2026
- **Type:** experimental
- **Primary category:** `hallucination-cascade`
- **Tags:** `error-propagation`, `hallucination-cascade`, `model-heterogeneity`, `metrics`
- **File:** `papers/hallucination-cascade/2606.07937_Hallucination_Cascade.pdf`
- **Main research question:** How does claim-level factual inconsistency evolve across sequential agent interactions, as a function of cascade depth and model heterogeneity?
- **Method:** 500 cascade experiments across 10 knowledge domains, 3 heterogeneous models (GPT5.3, DeepSeek-V3, LLaMA-3-70B-Instruct), 1,250 evaluated responses; claim-level factual inconsistency tracked per hop.
- **Main findings:** In 3-agent chains the normalized hallucination score DROPPED from 0.422 (first agent) to 0.272 (final agent), amplification factor 0.644 — i.e. net ATTENUATION, not amplification. However factual accuracy also declined (0.789 -> 0.769), revealing a suppression/accuracy trade-off.
- **Relevance to hallucination cascade:** The single most direct paper on the repo's core question — and an important counterweight: it is evidence AGAINST a naive 'cascades always amplify' story. Cite it whenever the amplification claim is made.
- **Limitations:** TODO: read full text. Note the reported model 'GPT5.3' should be checked against the paper body.
- **Verification status:** metadata-verified; findings from abstract

### Collective Hallucination in Multi-Agent LLMs: Modeling and Defense

### Reliability–Contagion Feasibility in LLM Multi-Agent Networks

- **arXiv:** `2607.21912` — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21912

- **Year:** 2026
- **Type:** theoretical+simulation
- **Primary category:** `error-propagation`

- **File:** `papers/error-propagation/2607.21912_Reliability_Contagion_Feasibility.pdf`
- **Main research question:** Can a network be simultaneously connected enough to be reliable and sparse enough to prevent error contagion?
- **Method:** Correction-aware SEIR-style network model (susceptible, exposed, infectious, corrected agents) with derived early-invasion condition for heterogeneous networks; coupled to an analytic majority-vote benchmark. 21,000 simulated trajectories.

### When Truth Is Distributed: Misinformation Derails Collective Fact Recovery in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems

- **arXiv:** `2608.03421` — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03421
- **Authors:** Chenfei Yan, Zeyang Yue, Feifei Zhao, Erliang Lin, Lu Jia, Haibo Tong, Mingyang Lyu, Chengyi Sun, Yi Zeng (Institute of Automation, CAS)
- **Year:** 2026
- **Type:** experimental+benchmark
- **Primary category:** `error-propagation`
- **Tags:** `misinformation`, `testimony-adoption`, `lineage`, `persistence`, `evaluation-framework`
- **File:** `papers/error-propagation/2608.03421_When_Truth_Is_Distributed.pdf`
- **Main research question:** How reliable is distributed information aggregation when one evidence holder is deceptive?
- **Method:** ForesightSafety-TIDE framework, strictly pairing all-honest collaboration against controlled deception by a key evidence holder. 120 five-agent object-movement environments where partial observations jointly determine a unique endpoint. 3 homogeneous MAS evaluated. Analyses multi-stage voting, testimony adoption, evidence-root lineage propagation, plus exit ablations.
- **Main findings:** Aggregate truth recovery collapses from 72.50% to 14.17% under a single deceptive evidence holder. A single false testimony is ADOPTED MORE READILY than truthful testimony, propagates to higher orders, and PERSISTS THROUGH HONEST AGENTS AFTER THE DECEIVER EXITS. Observers without first-hand evidence suppress incorrect consensus but do not improve truth recovery.
- **Relevance to hallucination cascade:** The clearest quantified demonstration that a bad claim outlives its source — the mechanism behind memory contamination and rollback failure. Anchors experiment-01 and experiment-03.
- **Limitations:** Deception is deliberately induced (not naturally occurring hallucination); synthetic object-movement environments; homogeneous systems only.
- **Verification status:** metadata-verified; findings from abstract
